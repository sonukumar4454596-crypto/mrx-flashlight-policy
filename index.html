MRX FLASHLIGHT

Privacy Policy

Package: com.sonu.mrxflashlight.apps • Last updated: October 11, 2026

100% Offline & Zero Data Collection

1. No Data Collection or Sharing

MRX Flashlight (developed by Sonu Lal) is built on a strict zero-data principle. The application does not collect, record, store, share, transmit, or sell any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or analytics. No user account, registration, or login is required to use any feature of the app.

2. Permissions Used and Why

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely to control the device's physical LED flash hardware via Android's CameraManager. No photos, videos, or camera previews are ever captured, recorded, or saved.
  • Accelerometer Sensor (TYPE_ACCELEROMETER): Used for the optional "Shake to Toggle" feature so you can shake your phone to turn the flashlight ON or OFF. Sensor data is processed in real time strictly on your device and is never stored or transmitted.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used only to provide tactile haptic feedback when toggling the torch, changing modes, or shaking the device.
  • Foreground Service & Notifications (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used only when you enable "Shake to Toggle" so that shake detection continues working reliably while the screen is off or the app is in the background.
  • Keep Screen On: Used to keep the display active while Screen Light mode is in use.

3. Permissions & Features NOT Used

MRX Flashlight does not request or use any of the following:

  • No INTERNET permission (the app cannot connect to external servers)
  • No third-party advertisements or ad networks
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-tracking SDKs
  • No location (GPS), contacts, microphone, or external storage access

4. Local Device Storage Only

User preferences (such as selected screen light color, brightness level, default mode, shake sensitivity, and vibration settings) are stored strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. Removing or uninstalling the application permanently deletes all saved preferences.

5. Photosensitivity Warning

The Strobe mode produces rapid flashing lights (1 Hz to 10 Hz), which may trigger seizures or discomfort in individuals with photosensitive epilepsy or light sensitivities. An in-app warning is displayed before activation. Please use caution and never point the LED light directly into anyone's eyes.

6. Children's Privacy

MRX Flashlight is safe for general audiences, is not directed at children under the age of 13, and does not knowingly or unknowingly collect any data from children or any other users.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page and inside the app with an updated "Last updated" date.
